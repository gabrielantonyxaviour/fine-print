# Tidewell Privacy Notice

Effective 1 September 2026

## 1. About this notice

Tidewell helps you find and book physiotherapy and GP appointments at independent clinics across the United Kingdom. This notice explains what personal data we hold about you, why we hold it, who we share it with and what choices you have. Tidewell Health Ltd is the controller of your personal data.

We take your privacy seriously and are committed to protecting it.

## 2. What we collect

When you create an account we ask for your name, email address, phone number and a password. When you book, we record the clinic, the time of your appointment and the reason for your visit, so the clinic can prepare. When you pay, you enter your card details on our checkout page. We also keep a record of the bookings and payments you make with us.

## 3. How we use your data

We use the details you give us to create your account, book appointments with clinics, take payments and send you service messages about your bookings.

### 3.1 Email

We use your email address to run your account and, only if you opt in, to send you news and offers.

You can change your newsletter choice at any time from your account settings.

### 3.2 Phone number

We use your phone number only to protect your account with two-step verification.

Each time you sign in, we text you a six-digit code to confirm it is really you.

## 4. Sharing

Clinics receive the details they need to see you, such as your name and the time of your booking.

### 4.1 Health information

We never share your health information, including the reason for your appointment, with advertisers.

### 4.2 Email address

We never share your email address with advertisers.

### 4.3 Service providers

We share personal data only with the service providers listed in section 7.

Each provider processes data on our instructions and under a written contract.

## 5. Security

We protect your data with encryption in transit, access controls and regular reviews of who can see what.

### 5.1 Passwords

We never store or log your password in readable form.

### 5.2 Card payments

We never store your full card number; payments are handled by Stripe.

### 5.3 Cookies

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

We set a single cookie that keeps you signed in, and it is removed when you sign out.

## 6. Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your account data while your account is open, so that you can see your bookings and receipts.

### 6.1 Deleting your account

When you delete your account, we erase your personal data within 30 days.

You can delete your account at any time from your account settings.

## 7. Our service providers

- Twilio, for sending sign-in verification codes by text message
- Stripe, for processing card payments
- Postmark, for sending account emails such as your welcome message
- Mailchimp, for sending our newsletter to people who have opted in
- Amazon Web Services, EU region, for hosting

## 8. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it or ask us to erase it. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

## 9. Contact

Email privacy@tidewell.example, or write to Tidewell Health Ltd, 14 Harbour Road, Bristol BS1 5TY. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
